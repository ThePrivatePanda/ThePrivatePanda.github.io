When to Compute in Space

with Dr. Gregory Falco · Cornell · 2025–26

Where should a computational task run — onboard flight computer, orbital data center, ground station, or terrestrial cloud — when latency, cost, energy, radiation, security, and reliability all conflict? Reframed a single-utility model as multi-objective trade-space search on the tier simplex; built the two-stage framework, both mission evaluators, and all six benchmarked optimizers behind the journal paper now under review.

Automated Functional Movement Screen

LISC, Dr. Silvia Ferrari · Cornell · Spring 2026

The analysis core of a VR posture-correction system driven by motion-diffusion and VLM feedback: from raw motion-capture quaternions, derives joint angles, ranges of motion, and clearances to grade the seven FMS physical-therapy exercises, scoring form 0–3 with a declarative rule interpreter whose guard operands reject biomechanical compensations.
