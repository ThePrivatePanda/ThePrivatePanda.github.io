About

I’m Parth Mittal — computer science at Cornell University (class of 2028), from Torrance, California.

I like systems work close to the metal and to the data: compiler passes and fuzzing infrastructure, visual-inertial navigation for drones that fly without GPS, deciding where a spacecraft should run its computation, and scoring human movement from raw motion-capture quaternions. The common thread is building the load-bearing part — the pipeline, the protocol, the solver — from scratch, and being able to say exactly why it works.

At Cornell I do research in two labs — space-instrument software with Dr. Savransky’s SIOSLab and motion analysis in Dr. Ferrari’s LISC — and I’m a co-author on two papers currently under review. Before that I led networks for a US Navy radio project through MIT Lincoln Laboratory’s SOCOM Ignite program.

Everything here ships under The Private Panda — the name my work has carried since before any of it was worth reading. Away from a keyboard: mountain biking, cycling, and reading.
